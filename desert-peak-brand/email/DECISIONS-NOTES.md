# Email signature — decisions (to merge into DECISIONS.md)

**Font stack: `"Helvetica Neue", Arial, sans-serif`.** Email cannot load web fonts reliably (Gmail, Outlook desktop and most mobile clients ignore `@font-face`), so the signature declares the system stack that sits closest to Archivo: Helvetica Neue on macOS and iOS, Arial on Windows and Android, both neo-grotesques with similar x-height and width to Archivo at 14 px. Georgia (the serif fallback declared for decks) is not used; the signature is all sans, matching the brand's UI voice, and a serif at 12–14 px in Outlook renders poorly. The stack is recorded in `collateral.config.json → fallbackFonts.email` and enforced by `verify-token-lock`.

**Outlook rendering needs a manual check.** The committed renders are headless Chrome; Outlook desktop on Windows renders HTML with Microsoft Word's engine, which no browser simulates. Before go-live, send the signature from classic Outlook and new Outlook on Windows to a Gmail and an Outlook.com account and look at both, in light and dark mode.

**Three things most likely to break in Outlook (Word engine):**

1. **The 2 px brand rule.** It is a table cell with `bgcolor` and `background-color`, `height:2px`, `line-height:2px`, `font-size:2px` and an `&nbsp;`. Word honours `bgcolor` but can enforce a minimum row height around 4–5 px and may render the rule thicker; it can also ignore `background-color` on the cell and paint only `bgcolor` (which is why both are set). If the rule disappears, the cause is an `&nbsp;` that Word replaced with a full-height space.
2. **The 2× logo scaling.** The PNG is 400×100 and displayed at 200×50 via `width`/`height` attributes and matching inline `width:200px;height:50px`. Classic Outlook respects the attributes; some versions scale by DPI setting (125 % / 150 % Windows scaling) and show the image at 250 or 300 px wide, breaking alignment with the 200 px rule. If that happens the fix is a 1× image at the same URL, not a change to the HTML.
3. **Dark-mode colour inversion.** Outlook dark mode lightens dark text and darkens light backgrounds. The ink text (`#221F1A`) becomes light, the brand-coloured links (`#923D28`) may be shifted to a lighter tint, and the `bgcolor` rule can be inverted to a pale line. The logo PNG is left alone, which is why it sits on its own padded surface panel with a hairline border: without that padding Outlook and Apple Mail render the transparent lockup directly on black and the ink wordmark disappears. The forced-dark render in `email/renders/*-dark.png` shows the expected result; Apple Mail without dark-mode support leaves the HTML untouched (dark text on the reader's dark background is then the reader's client, not the signature).

**Other choices.** One image only (the logo), no icon images for phone or mail: icons are the first thing Outlook turns into attachments and the first thing Gmail proxies. Links are `tel:` and `mailto:` only when the value is real; a `{{TODO}}` token is printed as text so a broken link never ships. Compliance lines are 12 px `ink-muted`, never smaller: 12 px is the floor in the type scale and the smallest size Outlook renders legibly. The TPMO paragraph is appended verbatim from `collateral.config.json` and the generator refuses to write a Medicare-track signature without it.
