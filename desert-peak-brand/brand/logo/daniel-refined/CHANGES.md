# Daniel's concept, refined — changes

> Website lockups with the brand wordmark (Archivo) in the horizontal arrangement of the Strata reference are in `web/` (see `web/CHANGES.md` and `web/comparison.html`).
> A redesigned version 2, with geometry, shapes and colours reworked, is in `v2/` (see `v2/CHANGES.md` and `v2/comparison.html`).

Source of truth: `client/daniel-logo-concept.jpg` (848×848 JPEG, raster only, flat fills). Generated by `scripts/build-daniel-refined.ts`; re-run it to regenerate every file here. Date: 2026-09-15.

## What was preserved (unchanged by design)

- The composition: thin ring, main peak with a snow cap, secondary peak behind and to the right, saguaro with one arm each side standing in front, two-line serif wordmark centred beneath. Same elements, same arrangement, same relative sizes (all measured from the JPEG and scaled 196/222 onto a 400-unit badge).
- The silhouette: peak slopes, ring diameter and stroke, cactus proportions, snow-cap depth.
- The wordmark content and hierarchy: "DESERT PEAK" large, "INSURANCE" small and wide-tracked in the warm accent.
- Ring stroke weight relative to the badge (6 units of 400, as in the original 6 px of 444).

## Changes, one line each

1. Traced to vector on a 6-unit grid (the ring stroke is the unit); anchors snapped, no stray nodes. Rationale: the only source is a JPEG.
2. Both peaks straightened into exact triangles on a single shared baseline. Rationale: the JPEG's edges wobble by 2–3 px.
3. Snow cap reduced from a ragged edge to three scallops. Rationale: the ragged edge turns to noise below 48 px; three scallops still read as snow.
4. Saguaro rebuilt from a trunk (24 wide, radius 12) and two arms (20 wide, radius 10), one corner radius throughout. Rationale: the original's arm widths and radii vary.
5. Saguaro grounded on the baseline (the original floats about 16 px above it). Rationale: optical alignment; a floating cactus reads as a mistake at small sizes.
6. Secondary peak kept, drawn at the measured apex and base; in single-colour variants it becomes an outline. Rationale: in one colour it would merge into the main peak and the silhouette would lose an element.
7. Spacing set in units: badge to wordmark 12 u, wordmark to INSURANCE 9 u, clear space 4 u, wordmark optically centred on the badge. Rationale: the original's gaps are arbitrary pixel counts.
8. Typeface substituted: Source Serif 4 Medium (weight 500, opsz 17), outlined to paths. Rationale: the concept's Garamond-style face is unidentified and unlicensed; Source Serif 4 is the brand's approved serif; Medium is the closest weight to the original. Visible cost: lower stroke contrast than the original's face.
9. Tracking kept at the measured ratios: 0.12 em on DESERT PEAK, 0.35 em on INSURANCE; kerning from the font.
10. Every colour mapped to the nearest brand token (table below); no new hue introduced.
11. Navy (#1F2A3C) → `ink`. Rationale: the palette has no blue. This is the most visible change and is an open item for Daniel.
12. Saguaro green → `positive` (agave, positive-700). Rationale: nearest token that passes 4.5:1 on the surface; positive-500 is nearer in hue but fails as a fill beside text. It reads teal rather than sage.
13. INSURANCE rust → `brand` (brand-600). Rationale: the nearest step (brand-500) is 4.3:1 on the surface and fails for small text.
14. Snow cap → `surface-raised`; ground → `surface`. Rationale: near-identical to the original values.
15. Single-colour variants (mono-dark, mono-light, 1-bit): the snow cap and the saguaro are cut out of the peak with a mask so the ground shows through, and the apex is kept as a 3-unit outline. Rationale: a flat one-colour peak either loses the snow or loses the cactus; the mask keeps every element's silhouette. The mask's white/black values are luminance, not paint.
16. 1-bit variant uses `currentColor` only, so it takes whatever single ink it is printed or faxed with.
17. All files optimised with svgo (preset-default, path precision 2, multipass); the optimisation is idempotent and produced no warnings; `viewBox` and `<title>` kept; no embedded raster, no live text, no styles.

## Open items for Daniel (not overridden)

- **Navy.** The brand palette has no blue, so the ring, peak and wordmark are near-black here. If the navy matters to him, it has to be added as a token, which reopens the locked identity; that is his decision, not a refinement.
- **The saguaro at favicon size.** Inside a ring inside a triangle its arms are under a pixel wide at 16 px and it disappears; at 32 px it is a smudge. Options: accept it (the ring and peak still read), or use a favicon without the ring and cactus, which is a different mark. Not decided here.
- **The snow cap behind a saguaro.** Left exactly as drawn.
- **The green.** The nearest legible token is a teal-leaning green (the palette's positive state colour). If sage is essential, that is also a new token.
- **The secondary peak** is barely visible in the colour version (as in the original) and is an outline in one colour. Confirm he wants to keep it.
