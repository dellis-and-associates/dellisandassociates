# Daniel's concept, version 2 — redesigned

Generated by `scripts/build-daniel-v2.ts` on 2026-09-15. Version 1 (the faithful refinement) stays in the parent folder; `comparison.html` here shows the original, v1 and v2 side by side at 256 / 96 / 48 / 32 / 16 px on light, dark and brand grounds.

## What stayed

The idea and its parts: a thin ring, a snow-capped peak with a second ridge behind it, a saguaro in front, and the two-line serif wordmark (DESERT PEAK over a wide-tracked INSURANCE in the warm accent). A viewer who has seen Daniel's badge recognises this as the same logo.

## What changed, and why

1. Peak moved off-centre and reduced (base 240 of 400 instead of 308) so it stands inside the ring with air around it. The original filled the ring edge to edge and looked cramped.
2. Saguaro moved from the summit to the front-left flank, standing on the same baseline. The original's cactus covered the snow cap; now both read, and the composition has a foreground, a middle and a distance.
3. Distant ridge kept behind and to the right, drawn as a lighter haze (`brand-400`) rather than a darker shade. Lighter-behind is how distance reads; it gives the badge depth without a second dark shape.
4. Snow cap redrawn as one line with three teeth instead of scallops. It reads as snow at 48 px and as a single notch at 16 px; scallops turned to noise.
5. Saguaro rebuilt with rounded elbows (arcs, not overlapping rectangles), arms at two heights as in the original, grounded on the baseline.
6. Ring stroke 8 units instead of 6. At 32 px the original's ring dropped below a pixel and broke up; 8 holds.
7. Peak recoloured from navy to the brand's red rock (`brand`, hematite). This ties the badge to the identity's meaning (the mountain is the rock) and to the rust INSURANCE line, which was previously the only warm element.
8. Saguaro in mid-tone agave (`positive-400`). Light agave vanished where the arm steps onto the light ground; the dark state green vanished against the rock; the 400 step holds on both (2.6:1 on each) and still reads as a green cactus.
9. Snow in `surface-raised`, ring and DESERT PEAK in `ink`, INSURANCE in `brand`: unchanged from v1.
10. Wordmark: Source Serif 4 Medium, tracking tightened slightly (0.10 em on the name, 0.36 em on INSURANCE), cap heights 40 and 16, gap 24. Marginally tighter than v1 so the wordmark sits closer to the badge's width.
11. New: `logo-mark-small.svg`, the peak and snow alone for favicons and any use under 32 px, where the ring and cactus cannot survive. This is the answer to v1's open item.
12. New: `logo-horizontal.svg`, badge at half size beside the wordmark, for site headers and email.
13. Single-colour variants: snow and saguaro are cut out of the peak with a mask, the apex kept as a thin outline, the ridge drawn as an outline; the part of the cactus standing outside the peak is filled so it does not vanish. Same approach as v1, verified on dark and brand grounds.
14. All files svgo-optimised (idempotent, no warnings), `viewBox` and `<title>` kept, no raster, no live text.

## Open for Daniel

- Navy is gone in both versions because the palette has no blue; v2 replaces it with the brand's rock red rather than near-black. If he wants navy, it is a new token and a decision about the whole identity.
- The distant ridge is optional. Without it the badge is simpler and the mark-small already omits it; with it the badge has depth. His preference.
- Recommended set if v2 is chosen: primary (stacked) for cards and social, horizontal for the site header and signature, mark-small for favicons and app icons.
