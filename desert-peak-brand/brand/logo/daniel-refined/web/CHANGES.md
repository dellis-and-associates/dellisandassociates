The v1 badge (ring, snow-capped peak, secondary peak, saguaro) set with the brand's own wordmark instead of the serif, and arranged like `brand/logos/logo-horizontal.svg` so it drops into the site header, email signature and social banners the same way the Strata lockup does.

## Changes from v1

1. Wordmark replaced with the brand wordmark, byte-identical paths from `brand/logos/wordmark.svg` (Archivo, "Desert Peak" title case over tracked INSURANCE), recoloured per variant. Rationale: the brand's approved display type, already outlined and tested; both lines in `ink` as in the reference.
2. Horizontal arrangement added, matching the Strata reference: badge left, text block vertically centred, badge sized 1.5× the text block so its ring survives at 40 px headers.
3. Ring stroke 8 units (v1 print uses 6). Rationale: at 32 px a 6-unit ring is under a pixel.
4. Badge geometry, colours and the single-colour mask are otherwise v1's, unchanged.

## Gates

Every fill is a token (`ink`, `ink-muted`, `surface-raised`, `positive`, `ink-inverse`, `currentColor`), asserted in the build; svgo idempotent with no warnings; no raster, no live text. Rendered at 24 / 32 / 40 / 64 / 96 px on light, dark and brand grounds (`tests/`, `comparison.html`): the wordmark reads at every size; the badge reads fully at 40 px and above, and as ring-and-peak at 24–32 px, which is the behaviour of the Strata mark's favicon variant too.

## Recommended use on the site

Header: `logo-horizontal.svg` at 40 px on desktop, 32 px on mobile. Footer and dark panels: the reversed file. Favicon and app icons: the badge does not survive 16 px (v1's open item); use `../v2/logo-mark-small.svg` (peak and snow only) or the Strata favicon until Daniel decides.

## Revision, 2026-09-19 (Daniel's notes)

Generated by `scripts/build-daniel-badge.ts`. Applied to all seven web SVGs and copied to the site's `public/brand`; the icon rasters and the banners were rebuilt from them.

1. **A beige disc behind the mountain**, `neutral-200` #DED6D0, filled inside the ring. The snow cap is near-white, so on the page colour it had almost nothing to read against; against the disc it stands out at every size down to 48 px. Run the script with `--disc neutral.100` for a lighter disc if this reads too strong in use.
2. **The small peak behind the mountain is gone.** It was the only element with no counterpart in Daniel's concept's silhouette and it read as a stray shape.
3. **The mountain is now clipped to the disc.** Not a request: with the disc filled, the mountain's base corners would have stuck out past the circle onto the page, and the base line already broke the ring. Clipping tucks it in and leaves the ring unbroken. The mountain itself is unchanged.

Everything else is byte-identical: ring, mountain, snow line, saguaro, wordmark, lockup geometry, viewBoxes. The single-colour and reversed variants keep the mask approach and take the same two changes; on dark grounds the disc resolves to the navy ground, so those files gain no fill.

## Wordmark re-cut in Figtree, 2026-09-19

Generated by `scripts/build-daniel-wordmark.ts`. The wordmark was still outlined from **Archivo**, carried over from the Strata identity, which made it a fourth typeface: the brand system is Instrument Serif, Figtree and DM Mono. Archivo and Figtree are both grotesque-adjacent, so in the header the logo sat next to the nav looking near-identical but not identical — a mismatch that reads as a mistake rather than a decision.

The wordmark is now outlined from **Figtree**, the text face: "Desert Peak" SemiBold, "INSURANCE" Medium. Nothing else changed — cap heights 27 and 10, tracking −0.012em and 0.22em, the two-line hierarchy, the badge, the gap and the vertical centring are the ones already in the artwork.

Instrument Serif was considered and rejected. It is the better-looking lockup at 96 px, but at the 48 px header size the INSURANCE line goes hairline, breaking the brand system's own "never below 28 px" rule for that face, and it puts the logo in the same voice as the H1 a few centimetres away.

**Widths changed**, because Figtree is narrower than Archivo. The site constants in `src/components/ui/logo.tsx` were updated to match:

| Lockup | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| horizontal | 342.51 × 69 | 297.44 × 69 |
| stacked | 250.12 × 270 | 205.05 × 270 |

Rebuilt from it: the five lockups, `wordmark.svg`, the site's `public/brand` copies and the four social banners. Unaffected: `mark.svg` and `mark-reversed.svg` (no type in them) and the whole `icon/` raster set.
